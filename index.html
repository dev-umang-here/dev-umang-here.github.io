About 01 / A little about me

I'm a backend engineer who owns the systems that move money. Four years in, I've gone from writing services to owning them end to end — the access model, the pricing pipeline, the alert that wakes someone at 3am.

How I Work Three operating principles

01

Ownership

Shipping a service isn't the job — being the one woken up when it breaks is. I design for the incident before it happens, not in the retro afterwards.

02

Measure first

Every rewrite I've shipped started with a number: a P95, a memory footprint, an MTTR. Change what the data says is broken, not what feels outdated.

03

Guardrails over autonomy

Propose, approve, execute. Every action my agents take is logged and gated. In production, trust is earned through auditability — not through the model sounding confident.
